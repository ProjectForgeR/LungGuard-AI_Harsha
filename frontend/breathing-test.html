Understanding Pulmonary Function Testing (PFT)

How spirometry parameters guide clinical evaluation of obstructive and restrictive lung disorders.

FEV₁ (Forced Expiratory Volume)

The volume of air forcefully exhaled in the first second of a forced expiration. A sensitive indicator of bronchial caliber and airflow limitation.

FVC (Forced Vital Capacity)

The maximum volume of air exhaled forcefully and rapidly following full inspiratory vital capacity. Reflects overall accessible lung volume.

FEV₁ / FVC Ratio

The fraction of vital capacity delivered in the first second. A post-bronchodilator ratio < 0.70 (70%) establishes persistent airflow obstruction under GOLD criteria.
